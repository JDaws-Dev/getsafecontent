Summer break is coming. That means your kids will have roughly 10 extra hours per day of free time — and a lot of that will involve screens.

You have two options:

1. Fight about it every day for three months
2. Set up a system now and enjoy your summer

Here's the system I use with my own kids. It takes about 30 minutes to set up, and it handles music, videos, and books automatically.

## The Problem with "Just Limiting Screen Time"

Most parents default to time limits. "You get two hours of screen time per day."

But here's what actually happens:

- Your kid spends those two hours watching whatever YouTube recommends
- They listen to music with lyrics you've never heard
- They grab books off the library shelf with no idea what's inside

**Time limits control the quantity. They don't control the quality.**

A summer media plan controls both.

**Summer-specific tip:** Let each kid build a "Summer Playlist" of approved albums. It gives them ownership and reduces requests.

## Step 2: Lock Down YouTube (10 minutes)

YouTube Kids is for toddlers. Your 8-year-old doesn't want Cocomelon — they want Dude Perfect and MrBeast.

But regular YouTube? That's a content minefield. The algorithm will take your kid from "cool science experiments" to conspiracy theories in about three clicks.

SafeTube lets your kids watch only approved YouTube channels. You pick the channels. They can't browse anything else.

**How to set up for summer:**

1. Sign up at [getsafetube.com](/signup)
2. Add channels your kids already watch (you'll be surprised which ones are fine and which aren't)
3. Use the AI Channel Review to quickly check new channels they request
4. Block the YouTube app on their devices

**Summer-specific tip:** Pre-approve some educational channels they don't know about yet. [Here are 25 great ones](/blog/educational-youtube-channels-for-kids). Mix in fun channels so it doesn't feel like school.

## Step 3: Set Up Book Screening (5 minutes)

Summer reading programs are great — until your kid picks up a "young adult" novel with graphic content.

SafeReads lets you scan any book's barcode and get AI-powered content analysis in seconds. Violence, language, sexual content, mature themes — all flagged before your kid reads page one.

**How to set up for summer:**

1. Sign up at [getsafereads.com](/signup)
2. Download the app
3. Before every library trip, scan your kid's picks

**Summer-specific tip:** Let your kids pick whatever they want at the library. Then scan everything at the checkout desk. If something's flagged, you can discuss it right there — "this one has some stuff that's more for high schoolers, let's find something else." No drama.

## The Daily Summer Routine

Here's what a typical summer day looks like with this system:

**Morning:** Kids listen to approved music while doing chores. No arguments about what's playing.

**Afternoon:** Screen time on approved YouTube channels. They can watch MrBeast, Dude Perfect, or that cooking channel — but not whatever the algorithm wants to show them.

**Evening:** Reading time. Every book has been scanned and approved. They're reading — not sneaking inappropriate content.

**Throughout the day:** Requests come in on your phone. "Mom, can I get the new Taylor Swift album?" Tap approve. Done in 10 seconds. "Dad, can I watch this new Minecraft channel?" Check the AI review. Approve or deny. No research required.
