My daughter had a school research project on ancient Egypt. Simple enough. She typed her question into Google, and within three clicks she was looking at something that had nothing to do with pharaohs.

That's the problem with giving kids access to a search engine that was built for adults. Google's SafeSearch helps, but it's not designed to think about what a child should or shouldn't be reading. It's designed to filter the most obvious stuff and hope for the best.

For homework, research projects, and curiosity-driven learning, kids need something better. That's why I built SafeStudy.

## What SafeStudy Actually Does

SafeStudy is an AI-powered search tool built specifically for children. When your kid searches for something, SafeStudy:

1. **Filters results through AI** — not just keyword blocking, but actual understanding of whether content is appropriate
2. **Blocks inappropriate queries** — if a child searches for something they shouldn't, it's caught before results ever load
3. **Logs every search** — parents can see exactly what their kids searched for and what was blocked
4. **Provides an AI tutor** — kids can ask follow-up questions and get age-appropriate explanations

It's not a locked-down baby tool. It's a real search engine that actually respects the fact that a child is using it.

## Why Google SafeSearch Isn't Enough

Google SafeSearch does one thing: it tries to filter explicit images and videos. That's it.

It doesn't filter:
- **Disturbing text content** — articles about violence, crime details, graphic descriptions
- **Age-inappropriate topics** — content that's technically "safe" but not suitable for a 9-year-old
- **Rabbit holes** — one search leads to another, and suddenly your kid is deep into something you'd never have chosen

And the biggest gap: **you never know what they searched for.** Google doesn't send parents a report. You have no visibility into what your child is exploring online.

SafeStudy gives you that visibility without hovering over their shoulder.

## How It Works in Practice

Here's what a typical homework session looks like with SafeStudy:

**Your kid opens SafeStudy** and types "what caused the American Revolution." They get clean, age-appropriate results. They can click into articles, read summaries, and even ask the AI tutor follow-up questions like "why did they throw tea in the harbor?"

**Meanwhile, you can check the parent dashboard** anytime. You'll see every search they ran, how long they spent, and whether anything was blocked. If something was flagged, you'll see exactly what it was.

No surprises. No awkward after-the-fact discoveries. Just a kid doing homework with a tool that was actually built for them.
